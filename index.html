Your safety matters

Work Smart.
Stay Safe Online.

Real opportunities take time and effort. Protect your privacy and be careful with offers that sound too good to be true.

🚩 Avoid upfront-fee promisesBe cautious if someone asks you to pay to unlock a job or guaranteed income.
🔒 Protect your detailsNever share passwords, verification codes, home address or private documents with strangers.
🔎 Check before you trustResearch the company, platform, reviews and payment terms independently.
🤝 Ask for supportIf you are under 18, involve a parent or trusted adult and follow each platform's age rules.
